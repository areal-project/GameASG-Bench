## Classification Notes

- **Arcade**: Fast-paced, skill-based games driven by scoring, in the tradition of classic arcade games.
- **Puzzle**: Games centered on logic puzzles, matching, or sequence-based problem solving.
- **Simulator**: Games that simulate real-world activities such as cleaning, dressing up, cooking, business management, and decorating.
- **Shooter**: Games centered on using weapons and shooting.
- **Racing**: Games centered on racing vehicles.
- **Sport**: Games that simulate real-world sports.
- **Platform**: Games centered on traversing environments by jumping, climbing, swinging, or similar movement.
- **Hack and Slash/ Beat em' up**: Open-world action combat centered on melee or ranged weapons.
- **Real Time Strategy (RTS)**: Battlefield strategy in which units and production systems on all sides advance simultaneously in real time.
- **Strategy**: Games requiring strategic thinking and planning to win, represented here by turn-based artillery combat.
- **Turn-based Strategy (TBS)**: Tactical strategy in which both sides follow a turn order to move, use abilities, and end their turns.
- **Visual Novel**: Interactive fiction centered on dialogue and narrative choices.
